# Work Type

A Work Type (StudioWorkType) categorizes tasks and labor lines by the kind of studio work involved:

- **carpentry** — woodworking and structural builds
- **metal_fab** — metal fabrication and welding
- **paint_finish** — painting and finishing
- **printing_graphics** — large format printing, vinyl, graphics
- **props_sculpt** — props and sculpture
- **rigging_install** — rigging, installation, hanging
- **transport_logistics** — delivery and transport
- **purchasing** — procurement activities
- **management** — project management overhead

Work types are used on tasks, work lines, material lines, and checklists to organize and filter production work.
